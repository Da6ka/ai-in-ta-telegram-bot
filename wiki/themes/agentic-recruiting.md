# Agentic recruiting

AI-powered agents are automating recruitment workflows end-to-end: sourcing, screening, interview coordination, and candidate communication. Unlike chatbots that draft text, recruiting agents actively interact with hiring systems (ATSs, scheduling tools, communication platforms), make candidate-facing decisions (subject to human review), and run 24/7. The defining 2026 shift is from isolated point automation to orchestrated, multi-stage agentic workflows that free recruiters for judgment-heavy work, and increasingly into end-to-end candidate logistics (housing, onboarding, compliance). A structural shift is also underway: as labor-force participation declines (particularly among 55+), agentic recruiting is reframing from a cost-optimization play to a structural response to long-term workforce scarcity, meaning automation planning must account for permanent, not cyclical, labor constraints. Josh Bersin's mid-year review confirms agents have "arrived" — Eightfold, ClearCo, Fusemachines, and others are shipping full suites in general availability, with early adopters reporting time-to-hire drops from two weeks to three days. International adoption is accelerating: Xobin's NURA agent is already deployed at major Indian IT services and banking firms, processing a million résumés in ten minutes. The risk is real: agent-driven screening, evaluation, and scheduling decisions are now live in production; pressure-test auditability and adverse impact before deployment.
