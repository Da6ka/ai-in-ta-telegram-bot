# Vendor Consolidation & Capital Flows

Capital is flowing to HR services roll-ups and enterprise-scale talent platforms, not just point-solution startups. Korn Ferry's $1.1B acquisition of UK RPO giant AMS, HR Path's $1B funding round for international expansion, and Mercor's valuation doubling to $20B within nine months all signal that the vendor landscape is consolidating around scale and integration. TA leaders should expect more vendor churn in outsourced layers and broader bundling of AI capabilities.

## Timeline

- **22–26 June 2026** — HR tech funding stayed active into late June, with HR software firm HR Duo among nine UK rounds totalling £166.65m in the week of 22–26 June, a 49% week-on-week jump — capital is still flowing to workforce tooling even as investors favour proven scale over moonshots. — [UKTN](https://www.uktech.news/funding/uk-tech-funding-roundup-partly-hr-duo-20260626)

- **29 June 2026** — Korn Ferry agreed to acquire UK-based RPO giant AMS from OMERS for ~$1.1 billion, creating a 16,000-person talent-solutions firm spanning RPO, campus/early-careers and contingent workforce. Expect accelerated AI-enabled RPO bundling — a prompt for TA leaders to reassess build-vs-outsource as the vendor landscape consolidates. — [Korn Ferry](https://www.kornferry.com/about-us/press/korn-ferry-announces-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-ams)

- **8 July 2026** — HR Path, the Paris-based HR consulting, technology-implementation and outsourcing group, closed a roughly $1 billion funding deal led by Ardian to fund international expansion, an active acquisition strategy, and a UK push — capital is now flowing to HR-services roll-ups, not just point-solution AI startups. TA leaders relying on outsourced RPO or implementation partners should expect more consolidation (and vendor churn) in that layer of their stack. — [BusinessCloud](https://businesscloud.co.uk/news/hr-path-targets-uk-acquisitions-with-1-billion-funding-deal/)

- **9 July 2026** — AI talent marketplace Mercor is in talks to double its valuation to $20B just nine months after its last round, and bought AI-agent-training startup Deeptune — a deal CEO Brendan Foody had personally angel-invested in three months earlier, "with a purchase already in mind." The valuation signals how much capital is flowing into AI-driven talent/labeling platforms, but the self-dealing disclosure is a governance red flag. Scrutinize conflicts and data provenance before routing sourcing or evaluation through fast-scaling AI hiring vendors. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/mercor-is-in-talks-for-a-20b-valuation/)
