[IBM Ventures Invests in Leena AI to Accelerate Agentic AI Across the Enterprise Back Office](https://www.hpcwire.com/aiwire/2026/10/21/ibm-ventures-invests-in-leena-ai-to-accelerate-agentic-ai-across-the-enterprise-back-office/) (21 October)

[Indeed Urges Human Oversight in AI-Powered Recruitment](https://mexicobusiness.news/cloudanddata/news/indeed-urges-human-oversight-ai-powered-recruitment) (27 October)
- The AI boom has quant and finance firms competing with tech for mathematicians and physicists, pushing some of finance's most secretive employers into unusually public recruitment drives. [AI talent war has quant firms racing to recruit top talent](https://www.staffingindustry.com/news/global-daily-news/ai-talent-war-has-quant-firms-racing-to-recruit-top-talent) (27 October)

[Double Nickel launches AI agent to automate driver employment checks](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/double-nickel-launches-ai-agent-to-automate-driver-employment-checks) (27 October)

[Hiring A New Generation Of Hardware Engineers With Colare](https://www.forbes.com/sites/davidprosser/2026/10/26/hiring-a-new-generation-of-hardware-engineers-with-colare/) (26 October)

## Worth Reading

- A report from Korea finds employers increasingly want workers who can check and correct AI's output rather than simply operate the tools, reframing what "AI skills" mean for hiring criteria. [Knowing how to use AI isn't enough. Companies want employees who can check its work.](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/knowing-how-to-use-ai-isnt-enough-companies-want-employees-who-can-check-its-work/12938201) (27 October)

**Bottom line:** As agentic recruiting tools and AI-native hiring platforms keep attracting capital, the recurring message — from Indeed to enterprise buyers — is to keep human judgment on the decision, so bake oversight into any deployment you greenlight.
