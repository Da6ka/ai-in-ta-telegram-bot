# Daily AI Recruitment Briefing — 29 July 2026

## Claude & Anthropic in TA
- Anthropic detailed bringing the **MCP 2026-07-28 spec** — the largest revision of the Model Context Protocol yet — to Claude, aligning connector authorization with OAuth 2.1/OIDC so MCP servers plug into enterprise identity systems like Entra or Okta without workarounds. For teams wiring Claude into ATS and sourcing connectors (Workable, Greenhouse, RocketReach), this is the governance layer that finally lets candidate-data access inherit your existing identity controls — re-check any assistant-to-pipeline integration against it. ([MCP 2026-07-28 spec: stateless core, coming to Claude](https://claude.com/blog/bringing-mcp-2026-07-28-to-claude)) (28 July 2026)
