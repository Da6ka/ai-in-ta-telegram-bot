# Daily AI Recruitment Briefing — 18 August 2026

## Claude & Anthropic in TA
- Anthropic is in talks to buy Israeli AI-infrastructure startup Decart for about $6 billion — its largest acquisition to date — to help its compute stack absorb surging enterprise demand ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/anthropic-said-in-talks-to-buy-ai-startup-decart-for-6-billion), 13 August). For TA teams running Claude in sourcing or screening, this is a capacity-and-latency signal worth tracking: Anthropic is buying infrastructure to keep pace as more workflows move onto its models.

## AI in Recruitment — What's New
- Private equity firm Silver Lake is in talks to take Workday private in what would rank among the largest software buyouts ever, sending the stock up sharply before a volatility halt ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/workday-skyrockets-25percent-before-trading-halted-on-report-of-silver-lake-takeover.html), 13 August). Workday owns Paradox and sits at the center of many enterprise TA stacks, so any leadership or roadmap shift under new ownership is a procurement risk to raise in your next vendor review.
- IBM and OpenAI announced a partnership to embed GPT-5.6, Codex, and ChatGPT Work into IBM Consulting Advantage across enterprise domains including HR ([IBM Newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-08-13-ibm-partners-with-openai-to-accelerate-secure-ai-deployment-for-enterprises-across-core-operations), 13 August). Expect the big consultancies to keep packaging AI directly into HR-owned processes — a reminder to define which recruiting decisions stay human before a systems integrator scopes them for you.

**Bottom line:** With Anthropic buying compute, Silver Lake circling Workday, and IBM wiring OpenAI into HR consulting all in a single day, the platforms underneath your TA stack are consolidating fast — pressure-test vendor stability and data terms now, not at renewal.
