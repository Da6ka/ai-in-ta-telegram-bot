# Daily AI Recruitment Briefing — 29 July 2026

## AI in Recruitment — What's New

- **Greenhouse CEO Daniel Chait warns of an AI "doom loop"** in hiring: candidates fire off AI-generated applications at scale while overwhelmed recruiters lean harder on AI to screen them — Greenhouse now sees ~254 applicants per posting and applications per recruiter up 412%. The near-term move for TA leaders is to invest in signal and fraud defenses (identity checks, structured evaluation) rather than more volume-filtering, or the arms race just escalates. ([Greenhouse CEO calls AI hiring an escalating "doom loop"](https://fortune.com/2026/07/27/greenhouse-ceo-daniel-chait-ai-doom-loop-job-seekers-spam-interview-applications-unemployment/)) (27 July 2026)
- **Gartner found 22% of CHROs say at least one leader in their org has stopped hiring for entry-level roles because of AI automation**, even as 95% have deployed AI but only one-in-five report significant value — and the roles being automated are exactly the low-complexity work early-career hires used to cut their teeth on. Before freezing junior pipelines, map whether AI is creating a skills-mismatch you'll pay for later, not just headcount savings today. ([Gartner Survey Finds AI Automation Is Reducing Some Entry-Level Hiring](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-7-27-gartner-survey-finds-ai-automation-is-reducing-some-entry-level-hiring-at-nearly-one-quarter-of-organizations)) (27 July 2026)
- **Revelio Labs launched an AI Labor Market Tracker**, a monthly real-time read on how AI is reshaping supply, demand, wages, and the hiring process itself — early data shows hires per job posting have fallen for six straight years and keep declining. It's a free external benchmark to pressure-test your own funnel metrics against the broader market before your next workforce-planning cycle. ([Revelio Labs Launches AI Labor Market Tracker](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/revelio-labs-launches-ai-labor-market-tracker-an-evidence-backed-real-time-measure-of-ais-impact-on-the-workforce-302836428.html)) (28 July 2026)

**Bottom line:** AI is squeezing the funnel from both ends — flooding the top with applications while thinning the entry-level roles at the bottom — so the highest-leverage TA investment right now is stronger candidate-signal and fraud defenses paired with a deliberate rethink of early-career pipelines, not reflexive headcount cuts.
