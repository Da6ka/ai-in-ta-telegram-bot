# Daily AI Recruitment Briefing — 4 August 2026

## AI in Recruitment — What's New

- **The EU AI Act's high-risk obligations for hiring tools became applicable on 2 August**, pulling recruitment AI — CV screeners, candidate ranking, interview scoring — into the top compliance tier with duties for human oversight, bias testing, logging and worker notice, and penalties up to €35M or 7% of global turnover; a proposed "Digital Omnibus" deferral is still in play, so anyone hiring into the EU should confirm the live deadline with counsel before assuming relief. ([The EU AI Act Is Here — What It Means for Employers](https://ogletree.com/insights-resources/blog-posts/cybersecurity-awareness-month-in-focus-part-iii-the-eu-ai-act-is-here-what-it-means-for-u-s-employers/)) (2 August 2026)
- **Workera launched proctored assessments** that watch candidates work through job-mirroring tasks and flag AI-assisted answers and tab-switching in real time, aimed at producing defensible, cheating-resistant evidence for high-stakes hiring decisions. As AI-generated applications erode trust in take-home work, it's a concrete option for teams trying to verify skill rather than screen for it. ([Workera Launches Proctored Assessments](https://hrtechcube.com/workera-launches-proctored-assessments-to-power-high-stakes-hiring-decisions/)) (28 July 2026)
- **Cognizant opened a dedicated EMEA AI Unit** to move agentic pilots into production, citing IDC data that 88% of AI-agent proofs-of-concept never scale — only four of every 33 reach live operation. For TA leaders planning autonomous sourcing or screening agents, it's a reminder to budget for the deployment gap, not just the demo. ([Cognizant launches EMEA AI Unit to help enterprises scale agentic AI adoption](https://news.cognizant.com/2026-07-28-Cognizant-launches-EMEA-AI-Unit-to-help-enterprises-scale-agentic-AI-adoption)) (28 July 2026)

## Worth Reading

- **ZipRecruiter's 2026 AI Employer Report ("More Jobs, Higher Bar")**, a survey of 1,000+ US employers, finds AI is expanding rather than shrinking teams — 35% expect it to raise total headcount — but the bar is rising fast: 74% now call AI skills a strong advantage or requirement, 57% have raised baseline productivity expectations, and 31% have lifted experience requirements for entry-level roles. The planning takeaway is to rewrite job requirements and screening rubrics around demonstrated AI fluency before the next hiring cycle. ([More Jobs, Higher Bar: The 2026 AI Employer Report](https://www.ziprecruiter-research.org/economic-insights-research/ai-employer-report-2026)) (29 July 2026)

**Bottom line:** Compliance and skills expectations are both tightening at once — verify your EU-facing hiring tools against the AI Act's high-risk rules while rebuilding requirements and assessments around provable AI fluency, not self-reported resumes.
