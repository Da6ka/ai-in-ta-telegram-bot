# Daily AI Recruitment Briefing — 23 August 2026

## Claude & Anthropic in TA
- Anthropic opened **Claude Academy**, a free learning hub built on how it trains its own staff, with ~20 self-paced courses spanning Claude 101 to API and agent development — including an "AI Fluency" course structured around a Delegation/Description/Discernment/Diligence framework ([Techgenyz](https://techgenyz.com/claude-academy-free-courses/), 20 August 2026). As "hire for AI fluency" moves onto req scorecards, this gives TA teams a no-cost, structured curriculum to build recruiter AI competence rather than leaving it to ad hoc experimentation.

## AI in Recruitment — What's New
- A new **Resume.org report finds 1 in 3 U.S. workers expect AI to run their company's entire hiring process**, with 57% of employers already using AI in hiring and 74% saying it improved hire quality — but more than half worry it screens out qualified candidates or lacks human oversight ([HR Dive](https://www.hrdive.com/news/ai-hiring-process/758384/), 19 August 2026). The oversight gap is the actionable part: document where a human stays in the loop before autonomous screening becomes your default.
- **Loxo expanded its Next-Generation platform with Loxo AI Chat**, a permission-scoped, zero-retention data-query tool that lets recruiters search their own database alongside a talent graph of 850M+ professionals and cite sources or abstain ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/private-companies/loxo-deepens-ai-driven-recruiting-platform-with-new-data-query-tool-and-expanded-agent-capabilities), 22 August 2026). It's a concrete example of the "agentic workforce" pattern arriving in mainstream ATS/CRM tools — worth pressure-testing the permission scoping and source-citation claims in any demo.

**Bottom line:** AI is moving from a feature to the operating layer of hiring — pair vendor capability (Loxo-style agentic search) with recruiter fluency (Claude Academy) and explicit human-oversight rules before candidates notice the difference.
