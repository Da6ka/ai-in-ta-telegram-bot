# Daily AI Recruitment Briefing — 11 August 2026

## AI in Recruitment — What's New

- **Match2's Connected Talent Solutions is now live on the SAP Store**, plugging a portable "universal candidate profile" and AI-powered matching into SAP SmartRecruiters and SAP SuccessFactors so employers can convert career-site traffic into opted-in, reusable talent networks instead of one-and-done applications. If your stack runs on SAP's TA suite, evaluate whether a consent-based portable profile lifts re-engagement of past applicants before you pay to re-source them. ([Connected Talent Solutions from Match2 Now Available on SAP Store](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/05/3339192/0/en/connected-talent-solutions-from-match2-now-available-on-sap-store.html)) (5 August 2026)
- **Refer, a Palo Alto startup with a candidate-pays model, raised $7.5M** (Canary-led, ~$10M total) for an AI agent called "Lia" that makes warm introductions between tech workers and hiring managers — free to employers, with workers paying 20% of their first paycheck; it has already set up nearly 6,000 interviews at 2,000+ companies. It's a signal to watch candidate-pays marketplaces as a low-cost sourcing channel, while scrutinizing how the incentive structure affects candidate quality and disclosure. ([Refer, a startup that charges workers for placement, raises $7.5M](https://www.staffingindustry.com/news/global-daily-news/refer-a-startup-that-charges-workers-for-placement-raises-75m)) (4 August 2026)

**Bottom line:** Both of today's moves attack the same pain — cutting through AI-inflated application volume to reach genuinely interested, verified candidates — so pressure-test whether portable profiles inside your existing ATS or a new candidate-pays channel gets you there more cheaply than buying more top-of-funnel.
