# Daily AI Recruitment Briefing — 28 August 2026

## Claude & Anthropic in TA
- **Anthropic-backed Ode acquired AI-services firm Casper Studios**, deepening the "forward-deployed engineering" model that embeds Claude into employee workflows and operational processes. Ode, the AI services company formed by Anthropic, Blackstone, and Hellman & Friedman, has acquired Casper Studios, an AI services firm specializing in deploying artificial intelligence into products and workflows — highlighting a crucial shift in enterprise AI spending from experimentation to practical implementation. For TA leaders, the signal is that the Casper acquisition gives Ode people who have already been working on the broader organizational side of adoption, where AI moves beyond a flagship application and into ordinary employee workflows — so define which recruiting decisions stay human before an integrator scopes your hiring processes. ([Anthropic-Backed Ode Buys Casper As AI Services Race Heats Up](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ronschmelzer/2026/08/21/anthropic-backed-ode-buys-casper-as-ai-services-race-heats-up/)) (21 August)

## AI in Recruitment — What's New
- **A high-touch, AI-native ATS deployment posted hard efficiency numbers in high-volume frontline hiring.** Alliance Building Services reported closing 35+ specialized, heavily regulated roles while reclaiming 70% of the manual effort previously consumed by sourcing and accelerating time-to-close by more than half, consolidating a fragmented software stack of spreadsheets, legacy databases, and disconnected point solutions onto a single AI-native applicant tracking system. The vendor's framing is worth pressure-testing in your own stack: conventional platforms provide standard ATS capabilities but lack autonomous outbound candidate discovery, pre-verified contact enrichment, and personalized AI outreach — an ATS is a system of record and a spreadsheet is a system of memory; neither is a system of action. ([HireQuotient Delivers 70% Reduction in Manual Recruiting Effort for NYC's Alliance Building Services](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/08/25/3350330/0/en/hirequotient-delivers-70-reduction-in-manual-recruiting-effort-for-nyc-s-alliance-building-services.html)) (25 August)

## Worth Reading
- **Voice and video AI are moving from add-on to core in hiring tools** — a shift to watch as you evaluate screening and candidate-experience vendors. The HR Executive team noted an uptick in voice tools during vetting of the 2026 HR Top Products; in some instances voice was an add-on, particularly in employee self-service, while in other cases voice and video were fully developed to power the product. The trajectory is steep: according to Gartner, 80% of enterprise software and applications will be multimodal by 2030, up dramatically from less than 10% in 2024. ([The next frontier of HR tech: Voice, video and multimodal AI](https://hrexecutive.com/the-next-frontier-of-hr-tech-voice-video-and-multimodal-ai/)) (27 August)

**Bottom line:** The through-line today is deployment over demos — from Anthropic's services push to a frontline ATS ROI case to multimodal AI going mainstream; press every vendor on where humans stay in the loop and what the integration actually automates before you scale it.
